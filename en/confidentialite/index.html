Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Data controller

The data controller is INWARD, SARL with share capital of EUR 1,000, whose registered office is located at Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, registered with the Paris Trade and Companies Register under number 981 066 145. Contact: info@inwardpro.com.

INWARD has not appointed a Data Protection Officer. Requests relating to personal data are handled at info@inwardpro.com.

2. Data collected

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect your first and last name, email address, company name, profile (company or individual), type of procedure, how you heard about INWARD and the information contained in your message. The public form must not be used to send identity documents or sensitive documents.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Responding to a contact or quote request: pre-contractual steps.
  • Arranging an appointment via Calendly: pre-contractual steps or performance of the contract.
  • Managing the client relationship and services: performance of the contract.
  • Issuing and keeping invoices: legal obligation.
  • Sending a newsletter: prior consent.
  • Measuring audience with Google Analytics 4, if enabled: prior consent.
  • Displaying YouTube videos or Google Business Profile reviews: consent where the technology used requires it.

4. Recipients and processors

Data may be accessed by authorised members of the INWARD team and by technical providers, including o2switch, Gmail or Google services, Monday.com, Calendly, Mollie, Google, YouTube and Lorem ipsum (newsletter).

5. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where necessary, INWARD checks that a recognised transfer mechanism and appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with the GDPR.

6. Retention periods

  • Contact requests without a contract: 3 years from the last contact.
  • Client data and files: for the duration of the contractual relationship, then for as long as necessary to comply with legal obligations and defend INWARD's rights.
  • Billing data: 10 years where required by law.
  • Newsletter: until consent is withdrawn or you unsubscribe.
  • Cookie consents: according to the consent management tool used and applicable recommendations.

7. Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability where applicable, or object to certain processing. You may withdraw your consent at any time. Contact: info@inwardpro.com.

You may also contact the CNIL (French data protection authority), 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, or www.cnil.fr.

8. Security and updates

INWARD implements reasonable security measures and limits access to the people and providers who need it. This policy may be updated; the date appears at the top of the page.
