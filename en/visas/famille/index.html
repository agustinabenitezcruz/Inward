Family visa

Would you like to join your French spouse or live in France on the basis of your family ties?

Depending on your situation, you may need a long-stay visa as the spouse of a French national, or a temporary residence permit marked "private and family life" for any other type of family tie.

Visa features

The features of each permit

Spouse of a French national

Spouse of a French national visa

  • Only available through marriage to a French national
  • Allows you to work in France
  • Issued for 1 year
  • Renewable from France
  • Immediate access to French Social Security rights
  • Access to the 10-year resident card (subject to conditions)
  • Opens the way to applying for French citizenship
Private and family life

"Private and family life" residence permit

  • Available through a PACS, marriage, as the parent of a French child or as the partner / family member of an EU citizen
  • Allows you to work in France
  • Renewable directly from France
  • Immediate access to French Social Security rights
  • Access to the 10-year resident card (subject to conditions)
  • Opens the way to applying for French citizenship

FAQ

The questions we are asked most often

01 Does a PACS automatically entitle you to a spouse of a French national visa?

No. A PACS does not automatically give access to the visa reserved for spouses of French nationals, which concerns married people. However, a PACS and the length of the relationship can be taken into account when assessing an application based on private and family life.

The decision depends on the applicant's overall situation and the supporting documents provided.

02 Where can I find official information on foreign nationals living in France?

General administrative information is available in the official section Étrangers en France on Service-Public.fr.

For visa applications, applicants should consult France-Visas. For some residence permit applications, the procedures are available through the ANEF.

03 Can the foreign spouse travel with a French residence permit?

Yes. Travel may be possible with a valid passport and a valid French residence permit. Conditions may differ when the permit has expired, when a receipt (récépissé) has been issued or when the application is still pending.

Before travelling, it is advisable to check the conditions for returning to France and the documents accepted.

04 How do you renew a private and family life residence permit?

The renewal must be prepared before the residence permit expires. The applicant must provide documents showing that the conditions relating to their family situation and stay are still met.

Depending on the case, the application can be made on theANEF or as indicated by the relevant prefecture.

05 What happens in the event of separation or divorce?

A separation or divorce can affect the renewal of the residence permit, but the consequences depend on many factors: length of the marriage, length of stay in France, living together, domestic violence, children or other personal and family ties.

It is advisable to have your situation reviewed before taking any step, as a separation does not automatically have the same effect in every case.

06 Can you bring your children to France with a spouse of a French national visa?

The children's situation depends on their family relationship, nationality, age and the status of the parent concerned. The spouse visa is for the foreign spouse; children may fall under a different procedure.

A full family review determines which applications should be submitted and in what order.
