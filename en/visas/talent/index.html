FAQ

The questions we are asked most often

01 How does INWARD maximise the chances of your visa application succeeding?

We maximise your application's acceptance rate with a method built on 4 key steps:

  • Tailored eligibility audit: we analyse your situation in detail to select the most suitable visa category.
  • A zero-defect application: we gather, check and organise every supporting document and administrative form to meet the exact requirements of the French authorities.
  • Strategic support: writing your business plan, formalising job offers or applying for a work permit: we secure the decisive steps of your procedure.
  • Rigorous follow-up until approval: we anticipate potential roadblocks, prepare you for consulate or prefecture appointments and handle the administrative follow-up until your residence permit is actually issued.
02 What are INWARD's fees?

Our prices are 100% transparent and given at the start of the service, with no additional costs. We offer case-by-case support for one-off needs, as well as packages for companies that want regular, ongoing follow-up of their foreign employees. You get full budget visibility from start to finish.

03 Why use an international mobility agency rather than handling everything in-house?

Immigration and visa procedures are all we do. Administrative regulations in France change constantly: as experts, we are informed in real time of every update and every change in the authorities' practices. Entrusting your procedures to INWARD avoids mistakes, speeds up processing times and frees your HR teams from administrative complexity, for zero-defect support.

04 How long does it take to get a work visa for France?
  • Passeport Talent (3 to 6 weeks): available to graduates from French institutions or for salaries from €59,000. It is the preferred route for fast hiring.
  • Employee / Temporary worker visa (4 to 6 months): for other profiles on permanent or fixed-term contracts, requiring a work permit from the authorities beforehand.

We analyse your candidate's profile from the very first conversation to optimise their start date.

05 What is the Passeport Talent and who is eligible?

It is a multi-year residence permit (valid for up to 4 years, renewable) designed to make it easier for highly qualified profiles to settle in France.

Who is eligible?

  • Qualified employees and graduates: recent graduates from French institutions, employees on assignment or employees earning from €59,000 a year.
  • Executives and corporate officers: legal representatives of companies established in France.
  • Entrepreneurs and project founders: founders of innovative companies or economic investors.
  • Researchers and artists: lecturers, researchers and artistic or cultural profiles.
