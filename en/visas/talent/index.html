FAQ

The questions we are asked most often

01 How can you obtain a Talent residence permit for yourself or your employee?

To obtain a Talent residence permit, you need to check that your profile matches one of the categories provided for, prepare the necessary supporting documents and submit your application according to your situation. The procedure may differ depending on whether you already live in France or are still abroad.

02 Which documents are required for a Talent card application?

The documents required vary depending on the category chosen. You may need to provide a valid passport, proof relating to your professional activity or project, and documents about your resources, housing or family situation. The exact list must be checked when the application is submitted.

03 Can you apply for a Talent residence permit from France?

In some cases an application can be made from France, in particular when the person already lives there with a compatible residence permit. The options depend on the applicant's administrative situation and on the Talent card category requested.

04 What if my employment contract or project changes?

A change of job, employer, activity or project may affect your Talent residence permit. Before any significant change, it is advisable to check whether a new administrative procedure or a change of status is required.

05 Where can I find the official conditions for the Talent residence permit?

See the up-to-date Service-Public page on the Talent residence permit: Carte de séjour pluriannuelle – Passeport talent.
