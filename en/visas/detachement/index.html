FAQ

The questions we are asked most often

01 Which visa do you need to work in France as a posted employee?

The visa depends on the type of posting. For a service provision between two independent companies, a short-stay visa may be required for an assignment of up to 90 days, while a long-stay visa marked "temporary worker" may be needed beyond 90 days. For an intra-company ICT transfer, a specific procedure applies, with a residence permit marked "ICT posted employee", "ICT trainee" or "employee on assignment" depending on the length and nature of the assignment.

02 What formalities must the employer complete before the posting?

Before the assignment starts, the employer based abroad must generally file a prior posting declaration through the SIPSI online service and appoint a representative in France where required. In some cases, a work permit is needed before the visa application.

03 Does a posted employee need a work permit?

Whether a work permit is needed depends on the length of the assignment, the activity and the employee's situation. Some audit or expert assignments of up to 90 days may be exempt, subject to the applicable conditions. The Talent Employee on assignment residence permit does not require a prior work permit either.

04 What is the difference between a posted employee and an employee hired in France?

A posted employee is temporarily sent to France by a foreign company to carry out an assignment or service. An employee hired in France is recruited directly by a French company under a French employment contract. The administrative procedure and the applicable residence permit may therefore differ.
