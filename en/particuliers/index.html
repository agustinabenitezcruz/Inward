FAQ

What individuals ask us

01 Which immigration procedures can you help individuals with?

We support you with visa applications from abroad, first residence permits, renewals, naturalisation, work permits, family reunification and DCEM (travel documents for foreign minors).

We also help you settle in France: access to Social security, getting a tax number, opening a bank account and having your driving licence recognised or exchanged.

02 Where do you apply for a residence permit in France?

Most residence permit and renewal applications, and some naturalisation procedures, are made online through the official ANEF portal (Administration numérique pour les étrangers en France).

However, each prefecture may apply its own rules and procedures. It is therefore best to check your prefecture's requirements carefully before starting, so you follow the right procedure and prepare the necessary documents.

03 Which documents should I prepare for an immigration procedure?

The documents required depend on your situation and the procedure. It is very important to provide every document that supports the reason for your stay in France, whether it is a job, studies, family reunification, a professional activity or any other project. A personalised check helps you put together a complete, consistent application that meets the authorities' requirements.

04 How much does support with an immigration procedure cost?

The fee depends on the type of procedure, how complex it is and the level of support you want. Once we have reviewed your situation, we can send you a personalised quote detailing the services included.

05 Why use specialist support?

Professional support helps you identify the right procedure, prepare your application properly, gather the necessary documents and avoid mistakes or omissions that could delay your application.
